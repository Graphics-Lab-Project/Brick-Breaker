## Spec notes
- Gun: one bullet at `(paddleX − 1, 312)`, ammo −1, mode → Normal when ammo hits 0, no cooldown.
- Laser: two bolts at `(paddleX − w/2 + 1, 308)` and `(paddleX + w/2 − 3, 308)`, cooldown 0.25 s.
- Projectiles move up 400 px/s; removed once their bottom is above y 0. Cooldown never goes negative.
