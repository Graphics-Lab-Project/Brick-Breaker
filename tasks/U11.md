## Goal
Short-lived polish effects spawned from engine signals: brick shards, +50 pop, muzzle flash, multi-split flash.

## Allowed files (the ONLY files you may change)
- qml/FxLayer.qml

## Interfaces (frozen)
- `qml/FxLayer.qml`: the Phase 0 stub's properties, signals, functions and the listed required `objectName`s.
- Do not change any interface. If one looks wrong, report BLOCKED (AGENTS.md section 4).

## Spec notes
- `brickBroken(r,c,tier)` → `breakFx` at `(c·48+1, (r+boardOffsetRows)·24+1)`: scale 1 → 0.6, fade, plus 6 shards (2×2) in `Theme.tierColor(tier)`.
- `capsuleCaught` → `scorePop` Text `+50` near the paddle, rising 24 px over 500 ms. `projectileFired` → `muzzle`; `multiBallActivated` → `splitFlash` at `ballPosition`.
- Keep `activeEffects` = number of live effect items; every effect destroys itself (≤ 600 ms). Nothing at all when `polish` is false.
- Timings: `docs/DESIGN_HANDOFF.md` §6 T1 table.
