## Goal
`GameEngine::stepBalls(h)`: move balls, bounce, hit bricks, score, speed-up/descent, lose balls/lives, detect level clear.

## Spec notes
- Per ball: integrate, `bounceWalls`, then the paddle (moving down + intersects): `paddleBounce` at the speed AFTER `m_pace.onPaddleHit()`, y = 319, `syncPace()`. If it just became Fast, rescale every ball to 260.
- Bricks: at most one brick per ball per step. Use `cellsOverlapping` + `ballHitAxis`, reflect, step back on the hit axis, `m_grid.hit`, `updateBrick`, +10 unless silver, emit `brickHit`, and if broken emit `brickBroken(r,c,tier)` + call `onBrickBroken`.
- Remove lost balls; none left → `loseLife()`. Otherwise `checkLevelCleared()`. Never re-implement the Phase 0 helpers.
