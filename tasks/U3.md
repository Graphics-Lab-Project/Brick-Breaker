## Spec notes
- Ball: 6×6 at `round(centre − 3)`. Fast trail = `ghost0..2` (opacity 0.55/0.30/0.15), visible only when `fast && polish`.
- Capsule: 20×10 body in `Theme.capsuleColor`, `label` 8 px text at x 23 with `Theme.capsuleName`. T1 pop-in: scale 0.6 → 1 over 100 ms, OutBack.
- Projectile: bullet 2×6 amber (`capGun`), laser bolt 2×10 magenta (`capLaser`).
