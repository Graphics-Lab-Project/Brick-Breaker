## Spec notes
- Board: Repeater over `bricks`, Brick at `(col·48+1, row·24+1)`, `y = boardOffsetRows·24` (120 ms slide when polish). `brickAt`, `flashBrick`.
- Repeaters: capsules (top-left = model x/y, rounded), projectiles, balls (`fast` when `engine.speedState === 1`). Paddle bound to engine.
- Catch flash: on `capsuleCaught(type)` show `flashTint` (capsule colour, opacity 0.18) and 24 px `flashName`, set `flashType`, hide after 500 ms.
- Wire T1 hooks (`brickHit` → `flashBrick`, `lifeLost` → `paddle.blink()`) when polish is on. Delegates must use `model.x`/`model.y` (roles shadow Item x/y).
