## Spec notes
- Implement the `// task "Engine core"` block of `GameEngine.h`. Use the Phase 0 helpers in `GameEngine_loop.cpp`.
- `startGame` (Menu/GameOver only): score 0, fresh `PowerState`, level 1, round 1, paddle reset, level loaded, ball on paddle; visible without a tick.
- `launchOrFire`: Ready → launch at `launchVelocity(motionDir or +1, pace speed)`; Playing → `fireWeapon()`. Ready ball follows the paddle (keys and pointer).
- Level cleared: after 1.5 s load the next level (10 → 1 and round+1), reset powers (keep lives), paddle reset, ball on paddle, `NextLevel`.
- Persistence through `openSettings()`: keys `paddleSpeed`, `acceleration`, plus `HighScoreTable::load/save`. Missing values → defaults. Save on every change.
