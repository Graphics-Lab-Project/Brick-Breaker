## Spec notes
- `onBrickBroken`: `maybeSpawn` at the cell centre (respect board offset) → `capsuleSpawned(type,x,y)`.
- `stepPowerUps`: capsules step → caught (+50, `capsuleCaught`, `applyCapsule`) → `capsuleLost` per missed one → weapons step → projectile hits → `checkLevelCleared()`.
- Projectile hits the alive overlapping cell with the largest row (ties: lowest col). Bullet: `destroy`, +50, `brickBroken`. Laser: silver absorbs it with no effect; else 1 hit, +10, `brickHit` (+ `brickBroken`). The projectile is removed either way. Any broken brick may drop a capsule.
- `fireWeapon`: `projectileFired(kind,x,y)` per new projectile, then `syncPower()`. Multi: replace all balls with 4 at ball 0's position using `multiBallVelocities(pace speed)`.
