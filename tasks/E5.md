## Goal
Capsule drop rolls, falling, catching by the paddle and removal when missed.

## Spec notes
- Drop: `rand(6) == 0`, then type = `rand(5)`. Only the injected `RandFn` may be used. No RandFn → never drops.
- Spawn top-left = brick centre − (10, 5). Fall 80 px/s.
- Caught = strict intersection with the paddle rect (touching is not caught). Lost = `pos.y > 336`.
