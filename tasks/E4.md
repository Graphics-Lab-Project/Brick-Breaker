## Spec notes
- Format: ≤ 14 rows × exactly 7 chars from `.123S`; invalid input → `false` and an empty grid.
- Silver (`S`): alive, unbreakable, hitsLeft 0, tier 0. `hit()` leaves it unchanged; `destroy()` (gun) kills it.
- Layouts: `docs/DESIGN_HANDOFF.md` §7 (also spelled out in the test). Levels loop: 11 → 1.
